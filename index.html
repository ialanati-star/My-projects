38 mm High-Power Rocket

Rocketry1,040 mm Airframe Dual DeployOpenRocket 3D PrintingEggtimer Quasar
Developing a 1,040 mm long rocket around a 38 mm minimum-diameter design, combining a sectional airframe, fin geometry, recovery-system packaging, and launch-rail guides. The project brings together CAD, additive-manufacturing preparation, and OpenRocket flight simulation.
The planned recovery system uses dual deployment with an Eggtimer Quasar altimeter. Motor options under study include H- and I-class motors; the supplied OpenRocket design also includes a J425 configuration. The J425 is a J-class motor, and the simulation represents a design study rather than a demonstrated flight.
